So v2_h16 and v2_h8 grow 5 B, ext shrinks 18 B and zst grows 21 B. This misses the strict "no worse
wasm" bar on three builds. The growth is LTO code layout: 2 functions change in h16/h8 (+2, +3 B),
3 in ext (+4, +3, -25) and 5 in zst (-25, +37, +4, +2, +3). It moves no gas row. Contract load is
about 1.1 Mgas per byte, so 21 B is about 0.02 Ggas, below the 1 Ggas the rows resolve
(zst load 186.4 -> 186.4).
Two other shapes were built and both were worse: `remove_inner(path: Path)` by value made
v2_h16 166175 B (+45). Moving the zero-sized decode into `remove_inner` without changing its
signature gave h16 166158 (+28), h8 166110 (0), ext 203234 (-39) and zst 170978 (-73), but it
reshuffles about 20 functions in h16 and loses the remove_step saving.
